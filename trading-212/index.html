first 90 days

I bring over 6 years of experience across growth, paid media, GA4, and GTM. Having managed $50K+ AUD/month Google and Facebook ad budgets while achieving 36x+ peak ROAS, 20% lower CPC, and 30% higher CTR, I focus on data-driven campaign scaling. Furthermore, I achieved 50% faster ROI measurement by engineering cohort tracking structures and multi-channel attribution models to guide growth spend.

  • 01Days 1–30

As Performance Marketing Expert, I will audit client-side GTM event triggers and data streams against server-side attribution endpoints to construct a martech gap matrix for commission-free stock and ETF campaign tracking. This audit establishes a baseline measurement audit to decide whether to route event signals through GTM container triggers or server-side CDP pipelines before expanding ad spend.
  • 02Days 31–45
    Instrument Multi-Channel Attribution & MMP Tracking — The role requires ensuring our martech stack enables best-in-class tracking, attribution, and reporting across Google, Meta, Apple Search Ads, X, and Reddit to drive retail investor growth. I will configure standardized conversion schemas within Google Tag Manager, GA4, MMPs, and CDPs to track user engagement across fractional shares, cash interest, and demo account features. This deployment delivers unified attribution dashboards to decide capital allocation and campaign structure across paid advertising channels.
  • 03Days 46–75
    Test Ad Creatives & Audience Bidding Strategies — Driving paid advertising efficiency for commission-free investing requires continuous testing of copy, creative variants, keywords, and campaign structures tailored to investor intent. I will establish a systematic A/B testing framework across Google, Meta, Apple Search Ads, X, and Reddit to evaluate ad creative concepts, audience segmentations, and bidding strategies. This experimentation cadence produces creative performance scorecards to decide which high-performing ad variants receive increased budget scaling.
  • 04Days 76–90
    Operationalise Growth Reporting & Campaign Scale — Supporting growth for extended market hours and wealth-building products requires scalable reporting pipelines that connect ad network spend with attribution metrics. I will automate daily acquisition reporting by integrating MMP and CDP data streams into executive dashboards for the Growth team. This operational framework establishes explicit CAC and attribution thresholds to decide when to scale successful campaign structures or pause underperforming ad sets.
💡 Day-90 decision gate
As Performance Marketing Expert, I will scale campaign spend across Google, Meta, Apple Search Ads, X, and Reddit when GTM, MMP, and CDP attribution pipelines verify clean conversion tracking for stock, ETF, and cash interest user acquisition. I will iterate ad copy, creative variants, keywords, audience segments, and bidding strategies when engagement metrics fluctuate while tracking instrumentation remains accurate. I will stop campaign execution if conversion attribution breaks, tracking telemetry fails across martech platforms, or customer acquisition costs degrade unit economics for retail investing products.

My name, Akshun, means "undestructable" in Sanskrit. That's not a personal brand line — it genuinely shapes how I move. I don't quit on campaigns, goals, or challenges like learning German from scratch after landing in a new country.

I started freelancing in digital marketing at 19 in India — working with Punjabi music artists, Australian businesses, and tech clients before I'd ever left the country. That early pressure to figure things out without a team or a budget is where the technical + creative hybrid came from.

I moved to Nürnberg in 2022 for an M.Sc. at FAU, added an Erasmus semester in Madrid, hit German C1, and picked up enough Spanish to embarrass myself. Outside the dashboards, I run a YouTube channel documenting the unfiltered reality of being an Indian expat in Germany — no filters, real stories.
