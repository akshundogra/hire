Why Eye-Able

Expanding into European markets requires signal-based precision, not generic list blasting.

Eye-Able achieved clear product-market fit and market leadership in Germany off the BFSG compliance tailwind. As expansion accelerates across international markets (Netherlands, France, Spain, Poland), top-of-funnel lists must be enriched with verified technical and organizational triggers so reps engage at the exact right moment.

⚡

The International Scaling Challenge

Because compliance needs span multiple industries and company sizes across Europe, every lead carries a distinct urgency. Scaling requires automated multi-market enrichment before records hit sales reps.

🎯

Signal-Triggered Opportunities

Reps shouldn't work static cold lists. They need automated signals — leadership updates, funding changes, website tech stack indicators, and compliance deadlines — delivered cleanly in HubSpot.

GTM Engineering Pillars

Systems I build to engineering standards, not just marketing checklists.

Primary Pillar: Negative-Signal Prospecting (Disqualification Crawler)

Instead of burning expensive enrichment credits on cold accounts, I build custom Python crawlers that inspect target accounts' HTML source code and privacy policies for existing accessibility vendor tags (AccessiBe, UserWay, EqualWeb, Recite Me, AudioEye). Accounts with active tools are filtered out immediately; unaddressed accounts become priority targets.
