  • 01
    Days 1–30: Measurement & Attribution Baseline — Audit tracking, lead sources, and 24-month historical pipeline across mdis-consulting.de.   • 02
    Days 31–60: Operating System & Lead Scoring — Deploy the tracking implementation plan and native lead/account scoring v1 in HubSpot using firmographic fit (50–500 DACH accounts) and intent signals (repeat visits, high-intent page behaviour, and identifiable SAP/ERP-stack signals). 📦 Deliverables: Live scoring v1 in HubSpot, routing SLA definition, and daily dashboard tracking target vs. actual SQL run-rate.
  • 03
    Days 61–90: First Controlled Test (ERP-Strategie 2030) — Launch the ERP-Strategie 2030 pilot campaign targeting SAP ECC accounts. Execute search intent capture, LinkedIn ICP demand creation, and GDPR-compliant outbound sequence with human-in-the-loop approval before send. 📦 Deliverables: SAP Strategy 2030 landing page, target account list, approved outbound workflow, paid/search test, documented success and stop criteria.
