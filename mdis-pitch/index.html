SLIDE 03 / 09 Commercial Challenge

The Top-of-Funnel Bottleneck

Sales converts qualified discovery calls well — the growth constraint sits entirely at the top of the funnel.

⚠️ The Core Bottleneck
SQL generation is the single factor limiting growth. Once a prospect becomes a Sales Qualified Lead (SQL), bottom-of-funnel conversion from SQL to closed contract is very strong. Partner referrals alone cannot sustain 20–30% YoY target growth.
🎯 The Landing Page Mandate
The primary job of the landing page is not just capturing generic emails (MQLs), but qualifying visitors into high-intent SQLs ready for discovery and architecture demo calls.
Core Objective: Drive self-sourced SQLs qualified by firmographics, ERP stack, and intent signals.
